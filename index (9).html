About Us

A Morbi accounting practice built on accuracy.

Patel & Patel Account has been providing account writing and accounting-related services to businesses in Morbi since 1999. What started as a dedication to careful, correct bookkeeping has grown into a long-standing local practice trusted by businesses across the town.
